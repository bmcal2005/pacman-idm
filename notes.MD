- Atari-HEAD uses full ALE action IDs: 0 NOOP, 1 FIRE, 2 UP, 3 RIGHT, 4 LEFT, 5 DOWN, 6-9 diagonals, 10-17 FIRE combos.
- Original repo mapped 1=UP, 2=RIGHT... so its Ms. Pac-Man labels are off by one. Factorized experiment was testing scrambled axes.
- 22 trials, 388,602 frames. Cardinals 90%, NOOP 4.8%, each diagonal ~1.2%, FIRE ~0.
- Old repo's "UP" (raw 1) = 4 frames total -> confirms off-by-one.
- Always-guess-a-cardinal baseline ≈ 90% accuracy, so accuracy alone is meaningless here.
- Pac-Man may not turn until he reaches an opening, so press and motion can be offset by a few frames.
- Phase 3: 7,942,089 params (matches original). Untrained loss 2.190 ≈ ln 9.
- Memorized 18 clips (2 per class) in 28 steps.
- Laptop CPU: ~4.5–11.5 s/step -> 80–200+ hours for 15 epochs. GPU required.
- BatchNorm gotcha: memorized batch = 18/18 in train mode, 2/18 (chance) in eval mode.
  Running mean/var lag behind fast weight changes. Harmless in full training.
  - Baseline 9-way, 15 epochs on T4: test acc 0.862, macro-F1 0.574, bal-acc 0.567. Best val F1 0.575 (val ≈ test).
- Lazy "always LEFT": acc 0.281, F1 0.049.
- Cardinals ~0.89–0.90 F1, NOOP 0.81, diagonals 0.17–0.23.
- Missed diagonals land on one of their two components ~80% of the time, almost never elsewhere.
- Hypothesis: a diagonal press in a corridor looks identical to the cardinal move -> action not observable.
- NOOP -> LEFT 17%: Pac-Man keeps moving with no input.
- Factorized (same everything, unweighted CE_h + CE_v), seed 42: test acc 0.869, F1 0.566, bal-acc 0.561, H 0.907, V 0.927.
- vs baseline: acc +0.007, F1 −0.008. Tie within single-seed noise.
- Diagonals: both models get exactly one half right ~80% of the time. Head design doesn't break the wall.
- UPLEFT 22% -> 10% = ~17 of 134 clips; treat as noise until replicated.
- Supports hypothesis: diagonal presses often not observable in the frames.
- Original repo also tied, but with scrambled axes, so it never tested this.
- Tracker: found Pac-Man in 97% of frames; motion matches label ~76–90% for cardinals.
- NOOP: Pac-Man stands still 94% of the time. But 17% of LEFT presses also show him still (blocked).
  ~635 still-NOOP vs ~610 still-LEFT clips -> visually identical -> explains NOOP->LEFT 17%.
- Diagonal presses: 68% move on one axis only, 31% both, 1% still.
- One-axis diagonals: model picks the SEEN half 70–74% vs UNSEEN half 11–14% (5–6x).
- Both-axis diagonals: exact rises 15% -> 26% (baseline), 13% -> 23% (factorized), but ~75% still called a cardinal.
